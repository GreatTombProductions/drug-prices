About This Data

NADAC (National Average Drug Acquisition Cost) is what pharmacies actually pay to acquire drugs from wholesalers and manufacturers. Published by CMS (Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services), it's surveyed weekly from a sample of retail pharmacies nationwide.

  • What this shows: The pharmacy acquisition cost per unit (pill, mL, gram). This is the wholesale cost — what the pharmacy pays before any markup.
  • What this doesn't show: What patients pay at the counter. Pharmacy markup, insurance negotiations, PBM spread pricing, and copay structures all add layers between acquisition cost and patient cost.
  • Brand vs. Generic markup: When a brand-name drug has a generic equivalent, we show the ratio. A 500x markup means the brand costs 500 times more to acquire than the identical generic molecule.
  • Price changes: Tracked across the data period (2024–2025). Shows whether acquisition costs are rising or falling.

Source: CMS/Medicaid NADAC Dataset (December 2025). 6,692 drug formulations, 1.6 million price records. Weekly survey of retail pharmacies.

The gap between what pharmacies pay and what patients pay is the information asymmetry. This tool shows one side of it.
